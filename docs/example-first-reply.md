# Example: interview → proposal

**Person:** owner of a small cleaning company (8 cleaners), not technical.
**Their message:** *"Clients text me all day to book, move or cancel cleans, and I juggle it in a paper diary. I'm
not technical. Could AI help? Where do I start?"*

Below is what Kickoff Compass produced in a test run (lightly trimmed). Tool prices were marked unverified in the run
itself; always check current details yourself.

---

## Turn 1: the interview starts

Think of me as the builder you've just hired. First, a short interview, so I build (or set up) the right thing and
don't waste your money.

**What I understood:** clients text you all day to book, move or cancel cleans, you keep it all in a paper diary,
and you want that to stop eating your day. *Example:* a client texts "Can we move Thursday's clean to Friday
morning?". Today you flick through the diary, work out which cleaner is free, text back, and rewrite the page.
Instead: the system sees the message, offers the client two free Friday slots, the client picks one, the diary and
the cleaner's day update by themselves, and you only get a ping when something looks odd.

*Assuming:* just you plus 8 cleaners, households or small offices, a modest monthly budget.
*Missing:* where you are, how clients text you, and how much control you want to keep.

**Honest first thing:** you very likely don't need anything built from scratch. Software made for cleaning
companies already does the diary part: online booking, a shared calendar, reminders, and a daily list for each
cleaner. I found several in third-party listings but haven't checked their own sites, so I'd verify that properly
in the first phase. AI could help with one thing those tools are weaker at: understanding a free-form text like
"any chance of Friday instead?". Whether you need that is something we test, not assume.

**1. What should we sort out first?**
- **A. Booking, moving and cancelling cleans, with one shared calendar for you and your cleaners (recommended):**
  this is what eats your day.
- B. Everything above plus quotes, invoices and payments
- C. Only a shared calendar, no automatic replies to clients

**2. Which country are you in?** *(No default: it decides which privacy and texting rules apply.)*

**3. Where do clients text you today?**
- **A. Normal text messages to your mobile (recommended guess)**
- B. WhatsApp
- C. A mix of both

**4. When a client asks to move or cancel, who makes the final change?**
- **A. The system offers free slots and updates simple moves, but you approve anything unusual, and everything in
  the first weeks (recommended):** a wrong change sends a cleaner to the wrong place.
- B. You approve every single change, always
- C. Fully automatic from day one (I'd advise against this at first)

**5. What monthly budget feels comfortable?**
- **A. Up to about £50 a month in total (recommended guess)**
- B. Under £20
- C. More, if it clearly saves you time

*Reply like "1A, 2: your country, 3B", or just "OK" to take my recommendations, but please answer question 2
directly.*

After a quick second round like this, I'll bring you a proposal: options, risks, a step-by-step plan, and rough
time and cost. I've saved where we are, so you can come back any time. Just say "continue my cleaning-bookings
project".

---

## Turn 2: they reply "1A, 2: UK, OK for the rest"

The AI acknowledges the answers, flags two things they raised (booking apps may need a separate business text
number; UK data-protection basics, marked "not legal advice"), and asks three new questions: cleaners' phone lists,
repeating vs one-off cleans, and keeping door codes out of the system.

---

## Turn 3: they reply "OK", and the proposal arrives

**Approaches**

| | A. Cleaning-booking app (recommended) | B. App + small AI helper | C. Build your own |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pros | Live in days; diary, reminders and cleaners' lists included | Understands any wording | Exactly what you want |
| Cons | Clients may need a link or a new business number | Extra setup and cost | Weeks of work; you maintain it |
| Estimate | 1-2 weeks, about £20-50/month | +£5-20/month | Not worth it for 8 cleaners |

**Plan**

| # | Phase | What you get | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Prep & checks | Today's numbers, 2 tools trialled | One week of booking texts counted; official-site prices noted; business-number question answered |
| 1 | Diary live (visible) | Cleans in the app, cleaners' phone lists | A moved clean shows on a cleaner's phone within 1 minute |
| 2 | Clients book, move, cancel | Clients use it, you approve changes | 2-week pilot with ~10 regulars, no wrong booking left uncorrected |
| 3 | AI helper (only if needed) | Free-form texts understood | 18 of 20 real past texts read correctly; nothing changes without your approval |
| 4 | Handover | One-page guide | You add a client and move a repeating clean using only the guide |

Plus risks with fixes, rough time and cost, and what's needed from the owner, ending with:
*"If this looks right, say 'go' and I'll start Phase 0. Or tell me what to change."*

After each phase, the owner gets a short report: **Done / Proof / Next / Needed from you.**
